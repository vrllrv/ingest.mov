What the numbers are

Search volume comes from Keywordtool.io, which reads Google Ads: a monthly average over the last 12 months, for Google Search only, in every interface language. It changes about once a month, so each market is measured once and refreshed after 28 days, not every day.

It is a floor on demand, not the market. Many people who need a DCP or an audio description never search for it: they ask a lab, a festival or a colleague. Read it next to the festival counts, which are the supply side.

How it's counted

  • Grouped spellings. Google reports one number for close variants ("dcp creation", "create dcp"). A keyword with the same volume, CPC and 12-month series as an earlier one in its service is marked grouped and counted once.
  • Worldwide has no location filter; each country uses Google's country location. Brand and tool names (FilmFreeway, DCP-o-matic) are measured in every market.
  • CPC is what advertisers bid for a click, in USD, weighted by volume. High CPC with low volume still means commercial intent.
  • Submitting filmmakers (proxy) measures searches for festival platforms and submission terms: a rough count of filmmakers in that market.
  • Not counted keywords are shown with their reason but left out of totals: broad or ambiguous terms (general sign-language searches, "digital cinema package", which Google merges with every meaning of "dcp"). The list is flagged in seeds.json.
  • Competitor & tool brands (Eventive, MASV, CineSend, Qube Wire…) are kept apart from the services: they size incumbents, not demand for yours.
  • Festivals are unique across Festhome, FilmFreeway, Shortfilmdepot, Festagent and Movibeta (same normalised name and country). Open now means the deadline hasn't passed. With email is the outreach list.
  • Russia has no market row: Google Ads doesn't serve it, so there's no country volume. Russian-language searches elsewhere still count in Worldwide.

Where it comes from

npm run keywords runs daily in GitHub Actions. It reads the shared Keywordtool quota first and never takes it below the floor, so the web app and MCP sessions keep their share. Markets, keywords and discovery heads live in ingest/keywords/seeds.json; the research log is ingest/keywords/RESEARCH.md.
