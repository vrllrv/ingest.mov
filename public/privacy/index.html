Privacy

Short version: no cookies, and we keep only what we need to answer you and deliver your DCP.

Who we are

ingest.mov is a business registered in Brazil under CNPJ 17.934.730/0001-62, based in Londrina, PR. Contact: dcp@ingest.mov.

What we store

  • Quote requests: what you type in the form (email, film title, runtime, master format, deadline, festival, message).
  • Orders: Stripe processes the payment and gives us your name, email, film title and file link. We never see your card details. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Where you came from: when you open our DCP page, order or send a request, we store your country (from your connection; we don't store your IP address) and, if you arrived from an ad, the search keyword. For orders and requests we also store the ad's click ID. This tells us which ads work.
  • Your film files: we download them only to make your DCP and delete them 30 days after delivery.

What we don't do

No cookies, no tracking pixels, no advertising profiles. Page visits are counted by the page itself (as above) and with Cloudflare Web Analytics; neither uses cookies or identifies you. We don't sell or share your details, except with the services named here (Cloudflare hosts the site and stores requests; Stripe handles payment).

How long we keep it

Requests and orders are kept for up to 2 years, or as long as tax law requires for invoices, and then deleted.

Your rights

Write to dcp@ingest.mov to see, correct or delete what we hold about you. If you're in the UK or EU you can also complain to your data protection authority.
